# Third-party assets

`src/main/resources/META-INF/resources/vendor/mermaid-12.1.0.min.js` is the Mermaid 12.1.0 browser bundle used by Daniel Oh's agentic-ai-java-workshop. The bundle retains its embedded third-party license notices. Mermaid is distributed under the MIT license. Upstream: https://github.com/mermaid-js/mermaid.

The diagram framing and solid-fill adapter follow the referenced workshop implementation: https://github.com/danieloh30/agentic-ai-java-workshop. The shared appearance uses a light paper background, solid pastel fills, and Mermaid's hand-drawn outlines. The vendored renderer is served locally; the SPA does not depend on a CDN at presentation time.
